Write a function `build_loaders(x, y)` that prepares data for training: `x` and `y` are NumPy arrays. Turn them into float PyTorch tensors, wrap them in a dataset, randomly split it into 80% training and 20% validation data (reproducibly), and return `(train_loader, val_loader)`, two `DataLoader`s with mini-batches of 16 samples. The training loader must reshuffle its data every epoch; the validation loader must not.
